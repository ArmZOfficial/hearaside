# Getting started

Choose what you hear in your headphones, separately from what viewers hear.

## 1. Install

1. Close your DAW and OBS.
2. Run the installer from the [download page](/download). It includes the plug-ins and the OBS source.
3. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

## 2. Add the plug-ins

| Plug-in | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Hub** | **Master**, once per project | builds the mix and sends it to OBS |
| **Track** | **last** insert on each track | chooses who hears the track |
| **App Audio** | the track you want to use | brings in audio from other programs |

<GuideScreenshot name="hub" caption="Hub brings all your tracks into one view" />

Plug-ins **above** the Hub on the master shape what viewers hear. Plug-ins **below** it only reach your headphones.

## 3. Choose who hears what

Every track gets two switches:

- **You hear**: in your headphones.
- **Viewers hear**: on stream.

For example, turn the backing track on for both mixes. Keep the click and guide in your headphones.

<GuideScreenshot name="track" caption="Track: the headphone button is for you; the broadcast button is for viewers" />

A track **without** HEARASIDE Track still reaches viewers through the master, but it isn’t in your headphones. Put HEARASIDE Track on every track you want to hear.

## 4. Check

Press **Setup check** in the Hub and follow any fixes it suggests. Next: [add it in OBS](/guides/obs).
