# Add it in OBS

The viewers’ mix goes from the Hub to OBS on your own computer. No virtual audio cable, and no internet needed.

1. In OBS, under **Sources**, press **+** and choose **HEARASIDE**.
2. Leave the bus on **Stream Mix** (or pick one of the eight stems).
3. The Hub shows the OBS status and the delay next to it, for example `DAW 256 · 5.3 ms`.

## Avoid hearing things twice

- The HEARASIDE source turns **Audio Monitoring** off when you create it. You already hear everything through your DAW.
- If your DAW also plays through **Desktop Audio**, turn Desktop Audio off in OBS, or viewers hear the DAW twice.

## Delay

HEARASIDE adds about one DAW buffer. The OBS source measures your DAW’s timing every 2 seconds and keeps its own buffer as small as is safe (around 11 ms at a 256-sample buffer). If it ever runs dry it adds 5 ms right away.

## Hotkeys

**Settings › Hotkeys › HEARASIDE**: *Mute stream* and *Hear viewers’ mix*. They work with a Stream Deck too. You need at least one HEARASIDE source in the scene collection.
