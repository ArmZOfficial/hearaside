# Record program audio

HEARASIDE App Audio brings the sound of one program, or the whole computer except the DAW, into a track.

## Play it in a track

1. Add **HEARASIDE App Audio** to an audio track.
2. Choose a program from the source button, or choose **Whole computer (except the DAW)**.
3. Use *You hear* and *Viewers hear* just like HEARASIDE Track.

<GuideScreenshot name="app" caption="Choose a program, such as Chrome, from the source button" />

Record program audio before an offline export. Programs play in real time.

## Record it with your DAW

A DAW records before the track inserts. Put App Audio on the **input channel** and switch **Program only** on.

### Studio One

1. Open **Song › Song Setup › Audio I/O Setup › Inputs**.
2. Press **Add (Stereo)** and name it `App Audio`.
3. Show **Inputs** in the Console and add the plug-in to this input.
4. Choose `App Audio` as a stereo track’s input and record.

### Other DAWs

- **Cubase:** use the **Input Channel**.
- **Reaper:** use the track’s **Input FX**.

Or choose **⋯ › Record** for App Audio in the Hub. Files go to `Documents\HEARASIDE\Recordings`; drag them into your DAW.
