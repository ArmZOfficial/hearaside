# Add it in OBS

Send the viewers’ mix from the Hub to OBS on the same computer.

1. In OBS, under **Sources**, press **+** and choose **HEARASIDE**.
2. Leave the bus on **Stream Mix** (or pick one of the eight stems).
3. The Hub shows the OBS status and the delay next to it, for example `DAW 256 · 5.3 ms`.

<GuideScreenshot name="hub" caption="Check the OBS status at the top right of the Hub" />

## Avoid hearing things twice

- HEARASIDE turns **Audio Monitoring** off for its source. Listen through your DAW as usual.
- Turn off **Desktop Audio** in OBS if it also captures your DAW, to avoid sending the sound twice.

## Delay

The Hub shows the measured delay next to the OBS status. It includes your DAW buffer and plug-in latency.

## Hotkeys

**Settings › Hotkeys › HEARASIDE**: *Mute stream* and *Hear viewers’ mix*. They work with a Stream Deck too. You need at least one HEARASIDE source in the scene collection.
