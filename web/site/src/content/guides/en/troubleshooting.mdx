# Fix common problems

Start with **Setup check** in the Hub: it lists what you hear, what viewers hear and what to change.

## Viewers hear nothing

- Is the Hub on the **master**, and is OBS showing a **HEARASIDE** source?
- Is **Stream muted** showing in the Hub? Press *Unmute stream*.
- Check that at least one track has **Viewers hear** on.

## I hear a track twice

The track plays through the master **and** your headphone mix. Put HEARASIDE Track at the **end** of the track (last insert), not before other plug-ins.

## A track isn’t in my headphones

Tracks without HEARASIDE Track reach viewers but not your headphones. Add HEARASIDE Track to it.

## The vocal and the music are out of time for viewers

Use **Sync** in the Hub: play the music, don’t sing, press Start and hold your headphones to the microphone for about 7 seconds. The Hub delays the music or the vocal for viewers so they line up.

## Still stuck

Press **Copy report** in Setup check and send it to us. It contains settings and timings only, never audio or track names you didn’t see on screen.
