# Getting started

HEARASIDE splits your DAW into two mixes: **what you hear** in your headphones and **what your viewers hear** on stream. It takes about five minutes.

## 1. Install

1. Close your DAW and OBS.
2. Run `HEARASIDE-Setup.exe` from the [download page](/download). It adds the VST3 plug-ins, the HEARASIDE source for OBS and cloudflared (for links over the internet).
3. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

## 2. Put the plug-ins where the sound is

| Plug-in | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **HEARASIDE Hub** | on the **master**, once per project | builds your headphone mix, sends the viewers’ mix to OBS, watches loudness |
| **HEARASIDE Track** | **last** insert of every track you want to control | the *You hear* and *Viewers hear* switches, levels, pan and delay for viewers |
| **HEARASIDE App Audio** | on any track | brings a program (Chrome, Spotify, Discord) or the whole computer into the track |

Plug-ins **above** the Hub on the master shape what viewers hear. Plug-ins **below** it only reach your headphones.

## 3. Choose who hears what

Every track gets two switches:

- **You hear**: in your headphones.
- **Viewers hear**: on stream.

A typical karaoke set-up: *Backing track* on for both, *Vocal* on for viewers only (you hear yourself acoustically), *Click / Guide* and *Talk mic* on for you only.

A track **without** HEARASIDE Track still reaches viewers through the master, but it isn’t in your headphones. Put HEARASIDE Track on every track you want to hear.

## 4. Check

Open the Hub and press **Setup check**. It tells you what you hear, what viewers hear and how to fix anything that’s off. Next: [add it in OBS](/guides/obs).
