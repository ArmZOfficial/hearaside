# Share a listen link

1. Open **Share and friends** in the Hub.
2. Turn the **listen link** on and press **Copy**.
3. Send the link to a friend. Keep your DAW open while they listen.

<GuideScreenshot name="share" caption="The listen link is on the right of Share and friends" />

- Your friend hears the viewers’ mix, sent directly from the Hub through a Cloudflare tunnel.
- The first time, Windows may ask to let your DAW through the firewall.
- Without cloudflared the link only works on the same Wi-Fi. The installer includes cloudflared.

## Links that stay the same

The link belongs to its project. If you sign in to the Hub, view or revoke it in [Account › Links and friends](/account/links).

## A friend sings with you

Press **Add friend** and send each person their link to join with a microphone. To process a friend’s voice in your DAW, add **App Audio** and choose the friend as its source.
